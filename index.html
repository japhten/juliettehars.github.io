About

Juliette Hars is a doctoral student at the CREATE Lab, EPFL. Her research focuses on using knitted and crocheted textile structures to create soft, conformal wearable sensors and actuator structures — combining textile design, materials, and sensing to create sensitive wearables and functional garments. (EPFL profile).

Current work explores fabrication methods, sensor integration, and characterization of textile-based sensing elements with the aim of practical wearable prototypes. Publications and technical reports are listed in EPFL’s research repository (Infoscience).
